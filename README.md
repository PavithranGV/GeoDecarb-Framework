# 🌍 Geo-Decarb: A GIS & Remote Sensing Framework for Steel Industry Decarbonisation  

## 📌 Overview  
The steel industry is one of the largest contributors to global CO₂ emissions.  
**Geo-Decarb** is a **GIS + Remote Sensing framework** that enables industries, researchers, and policymakers to spatially identify emission hotspots, optimise renewable integration, and design sustainable decarbonisation pathways.  

---

## 🚩 Problem Statement  
- Steel production is **highly energy-intensive** and relies heavily on fossil fuels.  
- Current monitoring of emissions is **fragmented, costly, and lacks spatial insights**.  
- Industries need **decision-support tools** that combine spatial data with decarbonisation strategies.  

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution  
✅ Use **Remote Sensing** to track energy use, land use, and atmospheric emissions.  
✅ Apply **GIS-based spatial analysis** to map steel plant clusters, emission hotspots, and transport links.  
✅ Integrate **alternative fuel & renewable energy siting** into spatial models.  
✅ Provide a **step-by-step decarbonisation framework** for the steel sector.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Build a **spatial framework** to guide decarbonisation strategies.  
- Enable **emission hotspot mapping** for better monitoring.  
- Support **integration of renewables** and circular economy models.  
- Deliver a **decision-support tool** for industry and policy.  

---

## 🛠️ Methodology  
1. **Data Collection** → Satellite imagery, steel plant data, transport network, emission records.  
2. **Spatial Analysis** → Hotspot mapping, carbon footprint zoning, renewable energy potential mapping.  
3. **Framework Development** → GIS-based decision tree for decarbonisation steps.  
4. **Decision Support** → Interactive maps/dashboards for policymakers and industry leaders.  

---

## 📊 Expected Outcomes  
- A **framework** for visualising and planning steel industry decarbonisation.  
- Clear maps of **emission hotspots** and renewable integration zones.  
- Spatial models for **carbon capture & waste recycling potential**.  
- A **roadmap** for industries to adopt greener practices.  

---

## ✨ Novelty of the Project  
- **GIS-first approach** → Unlike existing solutions, it visualises decarbonisation spatially.  
- **Integration of RS + GIS** for emission hotspot monitoring.  
- **Framework model** that industries can adapt for site-specific planning.  
- **Decision-support tool** rather than just theoretical recommendations.
